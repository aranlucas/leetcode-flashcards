---
title: Matrix
---

Matrix is an open source communication protocol intended to provide secure, reliable, and rich messaging and voice communication services. It provides a decentralized messaging platform with which users can securely communicate and collaborate with one another in real time. It can be used for instant messaging, voice and video calling, and other collaboration activities. Matrix enables users to easily create and join rooms, which are organized around topics or events. Rooms can be public or private, and they can be used to have open discussions or one-on-one conversations. Matrix also offers integrations with a variety of other applications, such as project management tools, calendars, and CRMs.
