---
title: Brainteaser
---

Brainteasers are puzzles or riddles that require a bit of extra thought to solve. They usually involve logic, lateral thinking, and problem-solving skills to answer. Brainteasers can come in many different forms, such as questions, visual puzzles, and codes. Brainteasers are typically used as educational tools to help improve problem-solving abilities, critical thinking, and creative thinking.
