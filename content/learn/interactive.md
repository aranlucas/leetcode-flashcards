---
title: Interactive
---

Interactive Voice Response (IVR) is a technology that allows a computer to interact with humans through the use of voice and touch-tone input. IVR systems use voice recognition, natural language processing, and touchtone signals to enable customers to interact with an automated system. Customers can use IVR to access services such as banking, call routing, and even weather forecasts. IVR systems are used in a variety of industries, including financial services, healthcare, travel, and retail.
