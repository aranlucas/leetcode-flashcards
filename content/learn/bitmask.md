Bitmask is an open source project that provides secure and easy-to-use end-to-end encryption for online communication. Bitmask is a privacy suite consisting of a VPN and an instant messaging client (IM). With the VPN, users can securely access the Internet without third-party tracking and eavesdropping. The IM client uses the same secure encryption technology as the VPN, ensuring that conversations are kept confidential. Bitmask can be used to bypass censorship and securely store passwords and sensitive data.
