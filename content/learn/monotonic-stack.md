---
title: Monotonic Stack
---

Monotonic stack is a stack data structure which allows efficient insertion, deletion, and lookup of elements in a monotonically increasing or decreasing order. It is a specialized form of a stack whose elements are always increasing or decreasing as they are added and removed from the stack. This data structure is advantageous in situations where elements are added and removed in a consistent order, as its performance is faster than a traditional stack.
