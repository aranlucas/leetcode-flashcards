---
title: Greedy
---

Greedy Algorithm is an algorithmic strategy that follows the problem-solving heuristic of making the locally optimal choice (the best solution at the time) at each stage with the intent of finding a global optimum. The algorithm has only one shot to compute the optimal solution and each sub-solution is not reconsidered. In simpler words, Greedy Algorithm builds up a solution piece by piece, always choosing the next piece that offers the most immediate benefit.
