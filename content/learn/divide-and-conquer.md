---
title: Divide and Conquer
---

Divide and Conquer is an algorithmic technique used to break down a problem into subproblems that are similar in nature. The goal of this technique is to divide a large problem into smaller problems that can be solved easily and quickly. The solutions obtained from the subproblems are then combined to get the solution to the original problem. The Divide and Conquer approach is used to improve the efficiency of an algorithm by solving the same problem multiple times with different inputs. Examples of Divide and Conquer algorithms are Merge Sort, Quick Sort, Binary Search, and Strassen's Matrix Multiplication.
