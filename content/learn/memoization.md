title: Memoization
questionCount: 32
---

Memoization is a technique used to speed up programs by storing the results of previous calculations and using them to avoid repeating costly computations. The technique is often used in dynamic programming, in which the same subproblems are tackled repeatedly. By storing the results from the subproblems, the results can be easily accessed in later sessions and a significant amount of time can be saved.
