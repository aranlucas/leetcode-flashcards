Line sweep (also known as sweep line algorithm) is a technique used in computer science and computational geometry for solving various problems. It works by placing a line (the sweep line) on a 2-dimensional plane in a parallel direction and then examining a set of objects ordered in their position along this line. Algorithms which use this technique usually maintain a balance between processing the objects and maintaining the ordered structure, thus allowing efficient handling of a variety of different problems. Common applications for line sweep algorithms include finding intersections between line segments and detecting points of interest in large data sets.
